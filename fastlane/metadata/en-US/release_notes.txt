LOGIT 5.1 pulls your whole progress into one Summary, rebuilds the workout recorder, and adds new ways to log what you train.

One Summary
This Week and Progress are now a single screen. Pick your timeframe once — 4 weeks, 3 months or a year — and every tile, chart and detail screen follows it.

Strength at a glance
A new Strength tile leads the Summary with your trend, and opens a full detail screen: your strongest lifts, the exercises driving the change, and a chart you can hold to inspect any point.

Muscle balance, rebuilt
The Muscle Groups overview now shows each group as a filling bar with its share of your sets, so you can see at a glance what you train and what you neglect.

Your week, on the Summary
The weekly goal is now an arc with your count inside, right in the Summary's title row. Tap it and the same arc opens full size, over your week in muscle colours, your streak and the next milestone.

A better recorder
The workout header folds away as you scroll and pulls back down with your finger. Supersets are a single card with the exercises paged inside it, sharing one action bar.

New ways to log
Track distance, distance + duration, weight + distance, duration-only and reps-only exercises. The exercise editor now composes the measurement from the fields you actually track, with a live preview.

Apple Health
Finished workouts write to Health, including a backfill of your history, plus two-way body weight sync and an active energy estimate for every workout.

More in 5.1
· History with week sections, per-section stats and filters
· Templates now match the workout design exactly
· Export everything you've logged as a file you keep
· Rest timer countdown in the Dynamic Island
· Deleting an exercise no longer touches your workout history
· Sharper charts, corrected pound rounding and better wording in every language
