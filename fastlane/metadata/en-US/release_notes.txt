LOGIT 5.3 shows you how every exercise is done, now in 3D, and makes the recorder fast.

See every exercise
All 208 built-in exercises are now performed by an animated figure, with the muscles they work highlighted. You'll see it in the exercise list, when you add an exercise to a workout, on Overall Strength and on every exercise's page. Tap it there to open the instructions, then turn the figure to see the movement from any side.

A faster recorder
The recorder no longer stalls after a tap. Closing a sheet, opening the exercise list, adding an exercise and deleting a set are now several times faster, even with years of history.
